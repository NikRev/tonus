About the app

Tonus builds a personal weekly workout plan from five quick questions: your goal, level, where you train, how much time you have and what equipment you own. Open the app and today’s workout is ready — with sets, reps, a rest timer and technique tips for every exercise.

No sign-up, no ads, no subscriptions. All your data stays on your iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tonus works without registration. All data is stored only on your phone.

How do I change my workout plan?

On the Plan tab, tap the swap icon next to any day to replace that workout. To rebuild the whole week, open Library → Programs → Find a Program, choose your goal and level, and tap “Apply to Plan”.

Why is the Activity tab empty?

Tonus needs access to Apple Health. Tap “Connect Apple Health” on the Activity tab, or open the Health app → your profile picture → Apps → Tonus and turn the data types on.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Data is stored only on the device. If you move to a new iPhone using an iCloud or computer backup, it moves together with the app.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app. Everything it stored on your iPhone is removed with it.

Which languages are supported?

Tonus is available in 35 languages. It uses the language of your iPhone automatically.

Is Tonus free?

Yes. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.
