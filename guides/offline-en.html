What works without a connection

Tonus stores workout plans, written exercise instructions and workout history on the iPhone. You can use the plans and written instructions without internet access. Apple Health integration is optional. Health data can sync with iCloud depending on your iPhone settings.

Why a video may still need internet

A video shown in the exercise library can be streamed or opened through an external provider. Online playback needs a connection. External RUTUBE and YouTube pages open at your request, use their own policies and may display ads. An offline plan does not mean every video is available offline.

Download a supported video from an exercise

While connected, open an exercise from the exercise library and find its video. If a download is available, choose “Download video” below the video. Wait for the “Offline” label and the saved file size. This control saves a supported exercise video; it does not download the YouTube or RUTUBE page.

Check playback before leaving the connection

After the download finishes, disconnect from the internet and reopen the exercise. Try playback before relying on it away from Wi-Fi. If the video still needs a connection, use the written instructions and check the download again when you are online.

Manage saved videos in Profile

Open Profile → Downloaded videos to see saved clips and their storage use. Swipe left on a saved video and choose “Remove video” to free that space. Your workout history is kept when you remove a downloaded video.

What sharing changes

You can choose to share a workout result or an aggregate usage report. The published description says that the usage report contains no Health data or identifiers. Sharing is a separate choice from keeping a local workout history. If you open an external video service, that service handles its own requests.

What to check on a new phone

Device storage does not itself promise automatic synchronization across phones. Before changing or resetting your iPhone, check your normal backup and transfer options. Keep this distinction in mind when comparing apps: offline access, local storage and synchronization are different features. Tonus 1.4 needs iOS 17 or later and works without an account or subscription.
